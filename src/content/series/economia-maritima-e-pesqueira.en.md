The series shows that the sea is not merely a natural or geographic space. It is an essential piece of economic infrastructure, connecting production, trade, food, energy, logistics, communities and territorial development.

**Maritime economics** analyses the activities associated with oceans, ports, transport, infrastructure, logistics chains, natural resources and coastal territories.

**Fisheries economics** investigates the use of fishery resources, the behaviour of the agents involved, the costs of the activity, how income is formed, the sustainability of stocks and the instruments of management and regulation.

## Where this series comes from

In the first semester of 2026 I had the opportunity to teach Fisheries Economics to a class in the Fisheries Engineering programme at the Federal University of Alagoas. Despite some health setbacks during the semester, the work was completed satisfactorily.

The experience taught me a great deal, brought me closer to students, researchers and topics related to fishing — and sparked a genuine passion for the field. Out of it came the interest in gradually developing research, teaching materials, scientific articles and outreach products on maritime and fisheries economics.

## Research and outreach

The publications in this series are in dialogue with ongoing research on marine heatwaves and fishing effort, on the effects of closed seasons and marine protected areas, and on Brazilian ports — all listed at the end of this page. The teaching materials from the Fisheries Economics course and outreach products aimed at the fishing communities of Alagoas and the lower São Francisco will be added here as they are completed.
