The repositories `tourism-gravity-brazil`, `ia-produtividade-tjs-brasil` and `pix-geography-brazil` were opened with code, tests, official pipeline outputs, `CITATION.cff` and data-licensing notes. The AI-and-productivity one also publishes the audited timeline of AI adoption by the 27 state courts as open data (CC BY 4.0). None of them contains the manuscript text.
